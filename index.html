UK SPL theory · Navigation

SPL Nav Practice

Three plotted tasks on the Southern England & Wales 1:500 000 chart (sheet 2171CD, Ed. 52). Each task has two 20-question papers with different wind and pressure, so the arithmetic is fresh each time. Topics follow the real paper: plotting and wind triangle, navigation technique, airspace, radio and rules, altimetry, and glide calculations.

Plot the task on your own chart first and measure everything yourself. Airspace answers are drawn from the UK AIP (AIRAC Oct 2026) rather than a scan of the printed sheet; where your chart or current NOTAMs disagree, they win. Pass mark 75%.
